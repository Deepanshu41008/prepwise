1. Structure of Question Paper

The Question Paper comprises four subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology. Each subject contains two sections:

  • Section A (Questions 1 to 35): All 35 questions are compulsory (+4 for correct, -1 for incorrect).
  • Section B (Questions 36 to 50): Contains 15 questions. Candidates may attempt any 10 questions. If more than 10 questions are attempted, only the first 10 attempted questions will be evaluated according to official NTA regulations.

3. Pacing & Time Allocation Strategy

To complete 180 questions comfortably and leave 15 minutes for final review, target: Botany & Zoology: 45-50 sec/Q · Chemistry: 60-70 sec/Q · Physics: 80-90 sec/Q.
